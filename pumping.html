Direction · Pumping stations

Pumping stations that run themselves

Manure and water pumping stations are small systems with expensive failures. OPERATOR control runs them unattended — by level, on schedule, with alarms before overflow, not after.

  • Level-based pump control: start and stop levels per pump, duty rotation for even wear.
  • Agitator cycles: scheduled mixing keeps solids from settling and crusting.
  • Emergency level alarm: high-level events are raised instantly, locally and remotely.
  • Dry-run and overload protection: current and sensor monitoring on every drive.
  • Full event log: levels, starts, alarms — visible in the dispatch interface.
