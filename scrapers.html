Scraper control that thinks about the alley

The scraper panel based on PLC Operator 27 drives 1–3 scraper drives with exact positioning along the alley. The system knows where every scraper is — even after a power cut.

  • Exact positioning: forward and return strokes stop where they should, every cycle.
  • Winter mode: automatic short strokes keep cables and chains from freezing in place.
  • Break and overload protection: current monitoring stops the drive before hardware is damaged.
  • Position register: scraper positions are stored in FRAM — after an outage the cycle continues, not restarts.
  • Motor-hour accounting: run time of every drive is counted and kept through power loss.
  • Schedules and logging: cleaning programs by time of day, with a full event history for the manager.

What the farm gets

Cleaner, drier alleys mean healthier hooves, fewer treatments and less labor on manual routines. The controller retrofits onto existing scraper drives — including DeBoer installations — without replacing the mechanics.
