Direction · Dispatch & analytics

Every barn system on one screen — and in your software

The platform turns motor currents, temperatures and cycle states into management data: milk yield context, hoof health and reproduction signals — not just blinking lights.

  • Central dispatch server: all controllers report to one server (PostgreSQL) with a web interface and remote access.
  • Open integrations: REST API connects to herd-management systems — GEA, DeLaval, DairyMaster — and to your BI.
  • Parallel parlor data tap: signals from milking rotaries are captured through galvanically isolated repeaters — analytics without interfering with the milking system.
  • Operator workstation (ARM): a full schematic of your facility with live states of every drive and sensor.
  • Demo access: ask us for a live demo of the dispatch interface.
